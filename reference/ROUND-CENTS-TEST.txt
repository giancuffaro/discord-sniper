PIVOTS % = share of all swing highs/lows that print at that cent ending.
OF-ALL-PRICES % = share of every 1-minute close at that cent ending (the baseline --
  nickels are already common quoted prices, so this is what a 'no effect' pivot rate looks like).
RATIO > 1x = that ending turns up as a pivot MORE than it turns up as a price at all (a real level effect).
RATIO ~= 1x = pivots just follow where price already sits most of the time -- no level effect.
