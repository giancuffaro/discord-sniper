============================================================================
THE ANTI-CLIP RULE — tight at first, then room to run
============================================================================

WHAT IT IS
  The tier rungs stay exactly as they were. On top of them, one rule:

     the stop may never sit closer than 40%% of the gain already made.

  That does nothing on a normal trade -- with a 5%% rung it only starts
  binding once you're up 12.5%%. Above that it takes over and keeps the
  stop a proportional distance back, so a runner is never strangled.

WHY PROPORTIONAL, NOT A THRESHOLD
  How much room a trade needs depends on how far it has already gone.
  A 15%% winner swinging 15%% is over. A 200%% winner swinging 15%% is
  breathing. A fixed rung can't tell those apart; a share of the gain can.
  It also means there is no "widen at +X%%" number to guess wrong.

WHERE 0.40 CAME FROM -- AND WHAT IT ISN'T
  520 simulated trades, nine names, real bars, then bootstrapped 4,000x.
  k=0.40 and k=0.60 both beat the old rule in 100%% of resamples and are
  statistically TIED with each other. 0.40 is the tighter of the two, so
  it gives back less on a reversal. Anything from 0.35 to 0.60 is the same
  decision. It is NOT a tuned optimum and should not be treated as one.

============================================================================
RESULT ON THE BACKTEST
============================================================================

                       total        win rate
  OLD 3.4.11           +2,436          20%
  tiers, no anti-clip  +2,872          31%
  tiers + anti-clip    +6,433          31%

  mean +7.69 per trade, t = 2.93 -- this one IS significant in dollars,
  where the plain tiers were not (t = 0.42).
  Beats the old rule in 100%% of 4,000 bootstrap resamples.
  Better on 95 trades, worse on 69.

  Per symbol, versus the old rule:
    TSLA +1,327   META +1,269   SPY  +787   NVDA +168   MSFT +159
    AMZN   +154   GOOGL   +76   QQQ   +56   AAPL   +1
  Nine of nine improved. Nothing got worse.

  THE POINT: it recovers the runners the fixed tiers were clipping WITHOUT
  giving up the win-rate gain. META went from +1,343 (clipped) to +3,589 --
  ahead of even the old wide rule's +2,320.

============================================================================
WHAT THIS STILL ISN'T
============================================================================

  Three quiet days, nine names, one market regime, and my IV model rather
  than real option quotes. The bootstrap proves the answer doesn't hinge on
  a few specific trades WITHIN this sample. It cannot prove the sample
  represents a fast tape, an event day, or your actual rooms.

  The median trade is -9 under every version of this rule, same as before.
  All the gain is in the tail. Your equity curve is still decided by rare
  big winners -- this rule just stops the ratchet killing them.
